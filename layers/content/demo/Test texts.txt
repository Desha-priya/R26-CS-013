✅ 🟢 Normal (Benign) Text Samples

These should ideally be LOW RISK

1

Hi team, please find the meeting agenda attached. Let me know if you want to add anything before tomorrow’s discussion.

2

Hey, can we reschedule our catch-up to next week? I’m tied up with project work today.

3

Good morning, the server maintenance is completed. Everything is running normally now.

4

Please review the latest project report when you have time and share your feedback.

5 (important edge case)

Can you please arrange a meeting with our boss today to discuss project updates?

👉 This one SHOULD be normal — but your model may wrongly flag it (like you observed)

🚨 🔴 Phishing / Malicious Samples

These should be HIGH RISK

1 (classic phishing)

Your account has been suspended. Please verify your identity immediately by clicking the link below or your account will be permanently locked.

2

URGENT: We detected unusual login activity. Enter your password here to secure your account now.

3

Congratulations! You have won $10,000. Click here to claim your reward within 24 hours.

4

Dear user, your bank account has been compromised. Login immediately to prevent unauthorized transactions.

5 (fake IT support)

This is IT support. We need your login credentials to fix a security issue on your account.

6 (advanced phishing — harder case)

Please review the attached invoice and confirm payment details. Failure to respond will result in service interruption.
